Before you enquire

How much does a switchboard upgrade cost?

Pricing depends on the existing board, circuits, required protection and any additional work. Request an assessment so we can provide a quote based on your property.

How do I know if my board needs upgrading?

Tell us its age, any problems and the changes you are planning. Photos can help with an initial assessment, but do not open the switchboard or remove covers.

Will the power need to be switched off?

An upgrade generally requires a planned power interruption. Our team will discuss the work and timing with you before it starts.
